Frequently asked questions

Why does EveryXL ask for access to a folder?

Mac App Store apps run in a sandbox and may only write where you allow. To save converted images next to the originals, EveryXL asks once per folder. Choosing a parent folder (for example Pictures) covers everything inside it.

Which format should I choose?

JPEG XL gives the best compression and can store JPEGs losslessly. AVIF creates very small files for the web. WebP and JPEG offer the widest compatibility.

A converted file is larger than the original. Why?

Already well-compressed images, screenshots or lossless settings can grow. Enable “Don’t delete or replace the original” or “Keep a copy of the original instead” in Settings → Encoders.

Is lossless JPEG → JPEG XL really reversible?

Yes. Use the “JXL → JPEG” mode to rebuild the original JPEG file bit for bit. Turn on “Verify Reconstruction” for an extra check.

How do I remove location data from photos?

In Metadata choose “Encoder – Wipe” or “ExifTool – Wipe”. Both remove GPS and other EXIF data.

Where are the logs?

Turn on “Write detailed logs” in Settings → Advanced, reproduce the issue, then use “Open Logs Folder” and attach the file to your report.
