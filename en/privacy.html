Privacy Policy

EveryXL · Last updated: 2026-11-11

Summary

EveryXL does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and makes no network connections.

Your images

Images you add are processed entirely on your Mac by encoders bundled inside the app. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Folder permissions

When you allow EveryXL to save into a folder, macOS gives the app a permission bookmark. It is stored only on your Mac and can be removed at any time in Settings → Advanced → Folder Access.

Settings and logs

Your preferences are stored locally on your Mac. Detailed logs are written only if you turn them on in Settings, stay on your Mac, and can be deleted from the same screen.

Children

EveryXL does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/ahmetozcan9211/everyxl/issues
